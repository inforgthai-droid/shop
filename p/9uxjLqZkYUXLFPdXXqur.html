The SRC (Super Racing Carbon) construction differs to regular carbon as it is designed to flex without splintering. Any flex in the outer is transferred to the multi-density inner shell which dissipates impact energy resulting in unparalleled protection for the wearer. The helmet shell is profiled to accommodate an FHR device and is fully compliant with the latest FIA 8860-2018 standard which is required for many international series.
