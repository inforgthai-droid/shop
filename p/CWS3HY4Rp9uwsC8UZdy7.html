420 grams bare weight Soft shoulder pads FIA 8858-2010 This Stilo HANS Zero is an ultra lightweight HANS/FHR device that weighs only 420 grams (bare weight). Superior comfort due to a raised lower collar 30º recline suitable for most single seater, formula cars and sports prototypes applications Soft shoulder pads for a comfortable fit Using a unique re-shaped design allows for ultimate weight saving whilst still retaining incredible strength and functionality.
