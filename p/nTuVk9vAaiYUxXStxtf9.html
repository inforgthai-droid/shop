Multi approvals for motorsport and karting Lightweight and super strong carbon shell Pinlock 120 fog resistant visor The Schuberth SP1 Carbon Evo Helmet offers a unique combination of lightweight design, solid construction, and top-tier safety for high-performance racing. Crafted with an autoclave-cured carbon fibre shell, this helmet is engineered for optimal aerodynamics, superior air circulation, and comfort, making it the ideal choice for many different motorsport disciplines. Discover the key features of the Schuberth SP1 Carbon Evo Helmet: Autoclave-Cured Carbon Fibre Shell: This lightweight and durable shell is handcrafted for strength and safety, ensuring a high level of protection without compromising comfort or performance. The result is a helmet that provides superior safety while minimising weight, crucial for high-speed racing environments. Wind Tunnel Tested Aerodynamics: Extensive testing has led to an aerodynamic design that reduces drag and turbulence. The perfect aerodynamic balance allows for maximum stability and reduced strain during high-speed performance, enabling smoother rides at top speeds. 12 Ventilation Channels: Strategically placed air channels in the shell and EPS provide efficient airflow, helping regulate temperature by drawing in cool air and expelling hot air. This ensures comfort and prevents overheating during intense racing conditions. Communication System Ready: The helmet is fitted with cable channels in the cheek pads, making it easier to integrate radio communication systems. This feature ensures that drivers stay connected during races without compromising safety or comfort. 3mm Thick 3D Injected Visor: Directly derived from F1 technology, the 3mm thick visor offers clear visibility, built-in fog resistance with a Pinlock® 120 lens, and tear-off buttons. The visor’s design contributes to enhanced safety by offering clear, unimpeded vision under varying conditions. SDL Dual Locking Visor Mechanism: This revised visor locking system uses a two-step closure that allows full closure or a controlled partially open position, is designed to be operated easily while wearing gloves, helps keep the visor securely closed in the event of an impact, and provides practical control over airflow so you can manage ventilation without compromising visor security during use. F1-Grade Visor Options: With twelve visor options, including various tints, coatings, and mirroring layers, the SP1 Carbon provides versatility. This allows you to select the ideal visor for different lighting and weather conditions, ensuring excellent visibility throughout the race (visors sold separately). Dual Motorsport and Karting Homologation: The helmet carries FIA 8859-2024 and Snell SA2025 approvals for motorsport alongside FIA 8878-2024 and Snell K2025 approvals for karting, confirming compliance with current safety standards across both disciplines and allowing you to use the same helmet across different categories without changing equipment or compromising regulatory acceptance. Pinlock 120 Lens for Fog Resistance: Equipped with a Pinlock 120 lens, the helmet’s visor provides excellent anti-fog performance, ensuring that your vision remains clear and unobstructed, no matter the conditions. FHR Compatibility with M6 Threaded Receptacles: Factory-installed M6 threaded inserts are integrated into the shell to allow retrofitting of FHR posts, which are available separately, enabling secure attachment of head and neck restraint systems and ensuring the helmet can be configured to meet series regulations. Tear-Off Buttons: The inclusion of tear-off buttons on the visor provides a quick and easy solution for clearing any debris or dirt that might obstruct your view, ensuring uninterrupted visibility during racing. GT Visor and Sun Visors Available Separately: A non locking- GT visor and short sun visor, available separately, are designed for use in closed cockpit race cars, offering enhanced protection and visibility in varied lighting conditions. Italian Craftsmanship: Each helmet is produced in Italy in the same factory that manufactures helmets for top-tier drivers, ensuring meticulous attention to detail and high-quality production. The Schuberth SP1 Carbon Evo Helmet is designed to provide the ideal balance of safety, comfort, and performance in competitive racing.
