Ultra light 970g carbon fibre shell FIA approved 3mm anti-fog visor Snell-FIA CMR 2016 certified safety standard The Stilo ST6 CMR Zero Carbon Helmet is built specifically for competitive karting where weight reduction, visibility, and comfort directly impact performance. Constructed entirely from high grade carbon fibre, it delivers a high strength, low mass shell that meets the latest Snell CMR 2016 safety standard. Drawing from Stilo’s Formula One development, it incorporates a redesigned shell layout, improved internal airflow, and a wider field of view to support consistent driving performance across a range of conditions. The shell system has been refined to reduce both physical size and weight, offering better aerodynamics and less strain on the neck during long sessions, all without compromising safety or structural integrity. Here are the features and benefits you can expect from the Stilo ST6 CMR Zero Carbon Helmet: Full carbon fibre shell construction: Manufactured using ultra-light, high-strength carbon fibre developed for Formula One structural and aerodynamic components, this shell delivers excellent rigidity and energy absorption while keeping total helmet weight to just 970g (XS shell), helping to reduce neck strain and improve comfort over race distances. Redesigned shell size system: The XS shell fits head sizes 53–56 and weighs 970g, while the S shell fits 57–59 and weighs 1010g, offering a compact fit with reduced external dimensions that improves aerodynamic performance and stability while enhancing driver comfort inside the kart. Double glazed 3mm FIA approved visor: Fitted as standard, the visor improves anti-fog performance and complies with the latest international karting safety regulations, ensuring clear vision across different weather and temperature conditions. Updated visor locking system: The revised locking mechanism secures the visor in place under impact or at high speed, providing a reliable seal and additional peace of mind during demanding sessions. Optimised ventilation channels: Internal airflow systems are designed to improve breathability and heat dissipation under the helmet shell, helping to keep your head cooler and more comfortable, even during long stints in warm or humid environments. Wider field of vision: The redesigned eyeport area has been engineered to offer increased downward and lateral visibility, improving track awareness and enabling quicker visual reference changes, which supports faster and more accurate reactions during racing. Aerodynamic accessory compatibility: The helmet supports optional front and rear spoilers (sold separately), enabling fine tuning of aerodynamic balance to suit different kart setups or track conditions. Coloured interior lining options: Available by special order in six colours - blue, yellow, red, pink, green, and orange, allowing for subtle personalisation of your helmet setup without affecting performance or comfort. This helmet helps reduce physical strain during racing while maintaining a high level of protection and stability at speed. Its low weight, compact shape, and tailored ventilation system make it suitable for maintaining comfort and focus over long sessions or during intense competition. As a high spec addition to your karting setup, the ST6 CMR Zero offers a practical and performance focused solution for drivers looking to get the most out of their equipment.
