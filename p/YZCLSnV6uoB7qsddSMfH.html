FIA 8859-2024 compliant: Meeting the latest FIA 8859-2024 homologation standards, this helmet is designed to absorb higher levels of impact energy, offering enhanced safety and protection for the wearer. Revised ventilation system: Six chin bar vents, along with crown vents and padding channels, ensure maximum airflow to keep you cool, improving driver focus even in the heat of competition. Wider aperture for better vision: The helmet’s increased downward vision allows you to maintain better awareness of your surroundings, contributing to both safety and performance during a race. Updated cheek padding: Re-designed pads offer a closer fit and improved stability, enhancing comfort while driving. The padding is customisable with different thickness options (available separately), providing a tailored fit for better support. Advanced padding technology: Special channels in the padding enhance airflow through the helmet interior, keeping you cooler and reducing fatigue. Removable sun visor: Designed to reduce glare, the sun visor improves your view of the road ahead, helping to reduce eye strain and keep you focused, with additional short visors available separately in different colours for customisation. Intercom and drink tube channels: Built-in channels provide the option to retrofit an intercom or hydration system, offering added convenience.
