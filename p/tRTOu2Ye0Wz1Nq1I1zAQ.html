FIA 8860-2018 Approved Lightest helmet in its class Improved field of view The Stilo ST6 GTN Zero Carbon Helmet offers ultra-lightweight and super-strong head protection thanks to its high tensile carbon fibre shell, designed to minimise strain on neck muscles. Free of electronics and side ports, this FIA 8860-approved helmet is the lightest in its class, featuring an improved field of view and revised cheek padding for added comfort during long races. Here are the key features and benefits that make this helmet stand out: Zero high tensile carbon fibre shell: Constructed using PURO technology, this shell eliminates the need for a clear coat, significantly reducing weight while maintaining exceptional strength, giving you the highest level of protection without adding unnecessary bulk. Up to 10% weight reduction: Compared to the previous ST5 GTN Zero model, this helmet is lighter by up to 10%, with weights starting from just 1120g (X-Small shell), providing even less strain on your neck muscles and improving overall comfort. No electronics or side ports: By eliminating these components, the helmet's weight is further reduced, helping to prevent muscle fatigue, particularly in long races. Revised ventilation system: Six chin bar vents, along with crown vents and padding channels, ensure maximum airflow to keep you cool, improving driver focus even in the heat of competition. Wider aperture for better vision: The helmet’s increased downward vision allows you to maintain better awareness of your surroundings, contributing to both safety and performance during a race. Updated cheek padding: Re-designed pads offer a closer fit and improved stability, enhancing comfort while driving. The padding is customisable with different thickness options (available separately), providing a tailored fit for better support. Advanced padding technology: Special channels in the padding enhance airflow through the helmet interior, keeping you cooler and reducing fatigue. Removable sun visor: Designed to reduce glare, the sun visor improves your view of the road ahead, helping to reduce eye strain and keep you focused, with additional short visors available separately in different colours for customisation. Intercom and drink tube channels: Built-in channels provide the option to retrofit an intercom or hydration system, offering added convenience. Multiple shell sizes: The helmet's sizing system has been revised with four different shell sizes, allowing a more precise fit across various head sizes for added comfort and stability during races. Whether you're in the thick of a long endurance race or tackling the demands of high-speed closed cockpit driving, the Stilo ST6 GTN Zero Carbon Helmet provides the highest level of protection while remaining incredibly lightweight. The advanced ventilation system keeps you cool allowing you to maintain focus under intense conditions. With its updated cheek padding and customisable fit, you’ll also experience greater comfort and stability, even during the longest and most demanding events. Experience the ultimate in head protection with the lightest FIA 8860-approved helmet available. The high tensile carbon fibre construction, combined with cutting-edge design, means you can race with confidence, knowing your helmet is engineered for peak performance, safety, and comfort.
