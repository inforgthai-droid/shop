FIA 8860-2018 approved Lighter than previous Evo III HP7 helmet New visor design for ultimate vision The Bell HP7 Evo IV Carbon Helmet is the result of a close collaboration with Formula one drivers and teams to develop a helmet which meets the demands of professional and club racers alike. Constructed using military grade carbon fibre composite and energy absorbing materials, the shell of the HP7 Evo IV helmet is incredibly strong, yet lightweight and thus exceeds the FIA8860-2018 advanced helmet standard for Motorsport. The stunning matt carbon gives this helmet a fresh and modern look. The HP7 Evo IV has a fantastic ventilation system with no less than 12 air intakes and extraction channels strategically placed around the shell for optimum levels of comfort with reduced fatigue Lighter than the previous Evo III HP7 helmet 'Smartlock' visor locking mechanism ensures the visor stays closed during impacts while also providing easy one handed operation Super high strength carbon shell combined with titanium hardware The interior features a ergonomic design which uses multiple densities of foam and padding to provide the best possible fit to various head shapes The visor mechanism locates the pivot points lower on the shell providing better aerodynamics and lower noise levels as well as improving energy absorption. A synthetic rubber visor seal ensure virtually no air or water can penetrate through the eye port. The visor itself is optical grade with varying thickness, exceeding 5mm in the field of vision and sets new standards in vision quality and anti fogging. Integrated channels offer provision for installing drinks tubes or communication systems. Previously only made available to top professional drivers, you can now for a small additional cost, customise your helmet interior too with a choice of lining colours. Standard off-the-shelf helmets are supplied with a grey interior colour, however the optional colour choices include White, Red, Purple, Blue, Green, Tan, Pink, Orange and Neon Yellow. Please note: All helmets ordered with a customised interior lining colour are taken as special orders only and, as such, are non-returnable. A 100%, non-refundable, deposit will be taken at the point of order. Please allow for an approximate lead time of 7-8 weeks for all customised helmets. FIA 8860-2018 Approved
