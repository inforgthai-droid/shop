The Stilo ST6 CMR Lite Carbon Kart Helmet is built for karting use, giving you a lightweight, compact, and stable helmet that meets the SNELL CMR 2016 safety standard for competitive environments. You get a Carbon P‑Matrix shell that provides the weight advantages of carbon with the cost efficiency of fibreglass, and this construction supports reduced strain during long sessions while maintaining the structural strength needed for repeated high-speed running. The helmet’s compact exterior, refined interior fit, and improved airflow work together to help you stay focused, comfortable, and consistent on track, especially when you are managing fast corners, heavy braking zones, and extended mileage. Here are the features and benefits you can expect from the Stilo ST6 CMR Lite Carbon Kart Helmet: Carbon P‑Matrix shell construction: Formed from a reinforced carbon based composite with controlled fibre layering, this shell achieves low overall mass without reducing rigidity, helping you manage neck fatigue across long stints and supporting stable performance during repeated accelerations. Compact dual shell system: Built with two dedicated shell sizes XS (53–56) and S (57–59) and reduced by approximately 10% in external dimensions, this streamlined shape improves aerodynamic flow and reduces turbulence around the head, helping you maintain steadiness through high‑speed sections. Formula 1 derived ventilation system: Developed using airflow data from F1 applications, the strategically positioned top ventilation holes guide air through the helmet to improve cooling and moisture control, helping you stay comfortable and alert in warm or demanding conditions. 3mm thick FIA‑standard visor: Produced using injection‑moulded material and certified to FIA 8860‑2018 standards, the visor includes antifog treatment, a washable inner surface, and a secure dual‑position locking mechanism, giving you consistent visibility during varied light and weather conditions. Improved crown and cheek padding: The redesigned interior supports even pressure distribution across the head, with enhanced cheek padding in the XS shell to reduce internal movement, helping you maintain a steady line through quick direction changes. Redesigned shell geometry: The updated shape reduces the frontal area and smooths airflow over the helmet, offering better stability when you are travelling at sustained speed. Extended downward field of vision: The revised aperture improves visibility toward the steering wheel and circuit surface, aiding precision in braking zones and corner entry. Personalised interior liner options: Colour matched liners available at no additional cost allow you to set up the helmet to your preference while maintaining the same level of padding support. Finish and graphic options: Available in a natural carbon finish or with graphic designs in blue, green, or red, the helmet gives you the choice to match your preferred look without compromising on construction quality, allowing you to customise your kit while maintaining the same performance and protection standards. The ST6 CMR Lite supports you in environments where weight, visibility, and ventilation directly affect your consistency and comfort across multiple sessions. You gain a helmet that manages heat, reduces strain, and maintains a stable fit, which helps you stay sharp when dealing with frequent direction changes or extended track time. As a practical addition to your karting setup, it offers a blend of low weight, structural strength, and tailored fit that supports reliable performance every time you head out on circuit. The Stilo ST6 CMR Lite Carbon kart helmet is available with a choice of interior lining colours. The standard 'off the shelf' helmets are supplied with a black lining.
