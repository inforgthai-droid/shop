The HANS Pro Ultra Lite Device applies lightweight construction of the Carbon-Fibre Pro-Series to the modern new shape of the HANS® Sport II, FIA 8858-2010 certified. The Ultra Lite saves even more weight on the Ultra by the way of 100g, making it the lightest FHR device on the market. Ideal for easier entry and exit in many smaller, tighter spaced vehicles. The aerospace grade, 100% Carbon-fibre construction features a totally hollow core for increased weight savings. The Ultra Lite also features sliding tethers which are standard for great visibility and soft shoulder pads are fitted as standard for additional comfort. The HANS Pro Ultra Lite are available with a 20-degree recline making them suitable for saloon or rally cars with a more upright seating position. Sliding tethers are fitted as standard to provide a greater freedom of movement.
