Ultra lightweight shell Snell-FIA CMR 2016 approved Bell have teamed up with F1 star Lando Norris to create this KC7-CMR karting helmet in fluro yellow and black. Norris worked closely with Bell's paint team to ensure the graphics and signature elements are closely matched to his own F1 helmet used in the 2024 season with the McLaren team. This Lando Norris edition version includes a 2mm multi layer green light smoke visor, top and side air intakes, rear spoiler and a high quality Bell helmet bag.
