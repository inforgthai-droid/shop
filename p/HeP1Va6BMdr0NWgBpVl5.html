FIA 8878-2024 & Snell K2025 approved Improved ventilation system Central visor locking mechanism The Arai SKV-R helmet is designed to provide enhanced head protection in motorsport karting environments through an advanced composite shell and refined impact management features. It uses a redesigned Complex Laminate Construction (CLC) shell with a lower visor mounting position to create a smoother upper surface that helps deflect impact energy more effectively. Discover the key features of this helmet: Complex Laminate Construction (CLC) Shell: A multi-layer composite shell structure engineered with carefully layered fibres and resins improves structural strength while maintaining controlled flex, which helps manage and disperse impact energy more efficiently and results in improved head protection during high-energy impacts. Lowered Visor Pivot System: The visor pivot point is positioned lower on the shell which allows a smoother and rounder upper surface profile that reduces snag points and helps impacts glance off the helmet rather than transferring force directly into it, improving overall energy dissipation. Lowered Visor Aperture Profile: The visor opening height has been reduced by approximately 17mm compared to the previous GP-7 design which increases the uninterrupted shell area above the visor, supporting improved impact performance in line with FIA test criteria and enhancing protection in critical impact zones. Extended Downward Field of Vision: The lower edge of the visor opening has been dropped to provide around 8mm of additional downward visibility which allows you to monitor dashboard instruments and controls more easily while maintaining full protective coverage. Dual-Sided Visor Lock System: A centrally mounted locking mechanism designed for operation from either side provides secure visor closure with a clear locking action, allowing you to open and close the visor reliably even while wearing gloves which improves usability during driving conditions. Advanced Ventilation Layout: Air intake vents have been repositioned approximately 15mm forward to optimise airflow channels through the helmet which increases cooling efficiency and helps manage heat build-up inside the helmet, resulting in improved comfort over longer sessions. Removable Interior Lining System: The internal padding is fully removable which allows easier cleaning and maintenance while also improving airflow circulation through the helmet, helping you maintain hygiene and consistent comfort. Adjustable Cheek Pad Design: Redesigned cheek pads include integrated openings for microphone installation and cable routing which simplifies communication system setup while keeping wiring neat and unobtrusive, resulting in a cleaner and more practical interior layout. FIA 8878-2024 and Snell K2025 Certification: The helmet meets the latest karting specific FIA 8878-2024 and Snell K2025 safety standards which confirms it has been tested to stringent impact, penetration and fire resistance requirements, giving you assurance of compliance with current karting regulations. You gain a helmet that improves how impact forces are managed while maintaining clear visibility of controls and stable airflow through the shell. Its design supports practical use through features such as easy visor operation, integrated communication routing and straightforward maintenance.
