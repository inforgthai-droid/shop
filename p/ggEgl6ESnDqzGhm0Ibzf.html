FIA 8860-2018 approved Ultra lightweight & super strong Compatible with Stilo wired & wireless intercoms The Venti WRX Raid Zero carbon helmet has been developed by Stilo to meet the extreme demands of off-road racing. This Zero version features the lightest and strongest shell in its class allowing it to surpass the stringent FIA 8860-2018 approval for maximum head protection. The helmet features a large visor aperture for an enhanced field of vision and the integrated intercom headset is borrowed from the Venti WRC rally helmet to ensure optimum communication clarity in either wired or wireless format. Developed for off-road racing but is suitable for many motorsport disciplines Wide visor aperture (34% larger than the Stilo ST5) offers an excellent field of vision Adjustable carbon fibre sun peak to reduce glare on bright days for better visibility and less eye strain Ultra Lightweight and incredibly strong 'Zero' carbon fibre shell for the highest levels of protection without putting unnecessary strain on the neck muscles, especially when worn for long periods Built in intercom headset provides the exception levels of communication clarity Stilo are known for. Noise attenuating ear muffs block out unwanted noise so you can focus on the intercom communication Adjustable noise cancelling microphone allows optimum distance from the mouth for the best communication Compatible with Stilo WRC or DG series wired or wireless intercom amplifiers allowing for a communication package to suit all requirements and budgets The Stilo Venti WRX Raid Zero 8860 carbon helmet is available in 2 different versions: Rally - This version is fitted with Stilo's WRC intercom system which includes: noise attenuating earmuffs which are fitted with speakers, magnetic-dynamic noise cancelling microphone with adjustable base, fully integrated Venti intercom plug which is compatible with wired or wireless intercom connection Turismo (Circuit) - This version is fitted with integrated radio connections, magnetic-dynamic noise cancelling microphone with adjustable base, noise attenuating earmuffs certified for circuit use. They are not fitted with speakers which are not permitted for circuit use - communication is always carried out through in ear speaker which are available to buy separately. The Stilo Venti WRX Raid Zero 8860 carbon helmet is manufactured incorporating cutting edge PURO technology which removes the need for clear coat to be applied. This reduces the helmet weight even more without compromising on safety. The top of the helmet is drilled for ventilation and can be fitted with a top air system to provide ducted air into the helmet. The helmet side ports are able to accept drinks tubes or side air systems. the helmet is supplied fitted with FHR posts. This helmet is designed to meet the demanding needs of racers in many motorport disciplines.
