Super strong and light weight FIA 8860-2018 & Snell SA2020 approved Noise cancelling microphone boom The Sparco Prime RJ-i Supercarbon helmet is an open face Motorsport helmet constructed from a high tensile carbon fibre shell reinforced with Carbon-Kevlar so it is lightweight, yet is still designed for maximum strength.
