FIA 8859-2024 & Snell approved Ultra light & strong carbon shell Multi density liner The Bell GT6 Ultra Carbon helmet has been developed for racers in a closed cockpit environment and shares the same modern and aggressive design as the HP6 Carbon helmet. The ultra lightweight and strong carbon fibre shell features a wide visor aperture for an increased field of vision and the ventilation slots can be connected to various external cooling systems if required. Ultra lightweight and strong advanced carbon shell 'Smartlock' visor locking mechanism ensures the visor stays closed during impacts while also providing easy one handed operation Multi density liner provides maximum energy absorption and impact performance Side or top ports can be purchased separately for connection to air vents or external cooling systems Extraction vents maximise cooling and airflow through the helmet Supplied with a clear visor with anti-fog treatment for excellent visibility in all conditions Factory fitted FHR / HANS posts The Bell GT6 Ultra Carbon helmet features an innovative 'Smartlock' visor locking mechanism which is engineered to ensure the helmet surpasses the stringent FIA 8859-2024 homologation. Unique to Bell helmets this system features a secure locking mechanism that keeps the visor firmly closed during racing and also if impacted during a crash. The simple push button release allows quick, one handed operations whilst eliminating the risk of accidental opening.
